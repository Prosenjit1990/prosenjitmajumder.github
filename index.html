About Me

I am a CNRS Postdoctoral Researcher at the FEMTO-ST Institute, Besançon, France. My research focuses on optical atomic clocks, precision spectroscopy, structured matter waves, twisted electron and ion beams, quantum optics, and optical metrology.

Previously, I worked at Tampere University (Finland), Durham University (United Kingdom), IIT Delhi (India), and LENS Florence (Italy).

My research interests span quantum optics, atomic and molecular physics, Rydberg excitation, optical frequency references, laser stabilization, and structured quantum matter waves.
